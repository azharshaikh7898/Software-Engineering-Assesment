# Acme Router X200 Quick Manual

## Setup

Connect the power adapter and wait until the status light turns solid green, which takes about 90 seconds. The default admin address is 192.168.8.1 and the default admin username is admin. The first login requires you to change the default password.

## Wi-Fi

The X200 supports Wi-Fi 6 on both the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands. The maximum number of connected devices is 64. The guest network is disabled by default and can be enabled in the Wi-Fi settings, with a limit of 8 guest devices.

## Reset and updates

To perform a factory reset, hold the reset button for 10 seconds until the status light blinks red. A factory reset removes all settings, including the Wi-Fi password. Firmware updates are installed from the Maintenance page; do not unplug the router during an update, which takes up to 5 minutes.

## Status lights

A solid green light means the router is online. A blinking amber light means the router has no internet connection. A solid red light means the device has a hardware fault and must be replaced under warranty.

## Warranty and support

The X200 includes a 2-year limited warranty. Support is available Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 5 PM IST at support@acme.example. The warranty does not cover damage caused by power surges.
