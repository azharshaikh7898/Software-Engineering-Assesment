# Acme Corp Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords expire every 180 days and the last 10 passwords cannot be reused. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and the source code platform.

Shared accounts are not allowed. Service accounts must be registered with the security team and rotated every 90 days.

## Devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access company email only through the managed mobile app. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 2 hours.

## Incident response

Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@acme.example, or by calling the 24/7 hotline at extension 7777. The security team acknowledges every report within 30 minutes. Severity 1 incidents, such as confirmed data exposure, trigger a post-incident review within 5 business days.

## Data handling

Customer data is classified as Confidential and may be stored only in approved systems. Confidential data must never be sent through personal email or consumer messaging apps. Backups of production databases are kept for 35 days and encrypted with AES-256.

## Vendor access

External vendors receive access for a maximum of 90 days. Access is reviewed by the system owner before renewal.
