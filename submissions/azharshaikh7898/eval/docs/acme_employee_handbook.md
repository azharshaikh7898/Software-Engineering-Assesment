# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Working hours and remote work

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Core hours, when everyone should be reachable, are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Remote work on Mondays requires approval from the team lead. Employees who relocate to a different country must inform HR at least 60 days in advance.

## Leave policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 8 unused days may be carried over into the next year; any further unused days expire on March 31.

Sick leave is 12 days per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Expenses and travel

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase. Receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD.

Employees may book economy class flights for trips shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours with director approval. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 150 USD per night in standard cities and up to 220 USD per night in London, New York and Tokyo.

The meal allowance during business travel is 60 USD per day.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 USD for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel is charged against the travel budget instead of the learning budget.
